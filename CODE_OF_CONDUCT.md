# Community standards

[Home](README.md) / Community standards

SecretBridge welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, experience levels and accessibility needs. Keep collaboration respectful, specific and focused on improving the project.

## Expected conduct

- Critique proposals and code rather than people.
- Explain objections with examples and actionable feedback.
- Give newcomers room to ask questions and correct mistakes.
- Respect privacy and the time of contributors and maintainers.
- Test only systems and data you are authorized to use.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, credential sharing and unauthorized testing are not acceptable. Do not pressure participants to disclose personal identity, private business information or confidential evidence in public.

## Raising a concern

| Concern | Channel |
| :--- | :--- |
| Non-sensitive moderation issue | Contact the maintainer through a GitHub issue |
| Concern involving private information | Request a private channel without identifying affected people |
| Software vulnerability | Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) |

Do not publish private evidence while requesting a reporting channel.

## Moderation

The maintainer may request edits, lock a discussion, remove harmful content or restrict participation proportionately. Decisions should be explained where that does not expose private information. Participants may request reconsideration respectfully through the same reporting channel.

These standards apply to project-managed spaces and project representation; they do not govern unrelated private activity.
